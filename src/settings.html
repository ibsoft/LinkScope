Database cleanup (maintenance)

When enabled, this policy overrides the existing history and application log retention periods. Cleanup runs when enabled or changed, at service startup and hourly thereafter. Active alerts and alerts awaiting email delivery are protected. Old pending SIEM log deliveries may be removed by retention. Deletion is permanent; back up your databases before enabling cleanup. When disabled, existing retention rules remain in effect, subject to the latest-month protection setting.
