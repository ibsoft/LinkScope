Checking standard ClamAV installation…

Pin the SHA-256 of a verified vendor binary. A locally calculated hash identifies bytes; it does not prove vendor authenticity. Verify the package or binary before accepting the suggested pin. Automatic actions verify the connected loopback peer and executable pin. Existing pins are never silently changed. Update the pin after trusted engine upgrades; otherwise response is refused.
